Privacy policy

Version 1.7 · effective from 3 November 2026 · This is a translation for convenience. The binding version is the Hebrew one; if there is any difference, the Hebrew version prevails.

This policy explains what information is kept, where, and why, in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and the regulations under it.

Operator of the app and controller of the database: לי בטאן (Lee Batan), email lybtn48@gmail.com.

Pay data is information of special sensitivity under the Protection of Privacy Law (Amendment 13), and we treat it that way: it is kept separately for each user, the database security rules block other users from accessing it, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

1. What information is kept

  • Information you enter: shifts, roles and rates, vacation and sick days, additions and deductions, calculation settings, payslip data you confirmed, the locality you chose (for Shabbat and holiday times and for the sky picture), and optionally: name, date of birth and gender. The date of birth is used for calculations that depend on age (minimum wage, youth employment law, tax and pension), for a birthday greeting, and if you agreed to the statistics, for an age group only. Gender is used for the mandatory pension age and for the tax credit point.
  • The shift log: a record of every start, end, addition, edit and deletion of a shift, with the time on the phone, so that you can show what was recorded and when. It is kept on the phone and in your account like the shifts, and is deleted together with the account.
  • Account details: user ID and email address, and if you signed in with Google, also your name and profile picture, for signing in and display only.
  • Signing in with an email and a password (if you chose it): your email address. Firebase's sign-in system (Google) keeps the password encrypted, and the app never sees it. As a guest, without an account, none of this is kept and your data stays on the phone only.
  • Technical information: IP address and browser details recorded automatically by the infrastructure providers when the App loads. The App has no ads, tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools.
  • Usage statistics with no identifying details (only if you agreed): they are off until you agree. Once a day one record per device is saved in the cloud, under a random ID not linked to the account: the App version, the type of installation, the first and last day of use, the number of days of use, the number of shifts and payslips, an age group (under or over 18), whether it is an iPhone (yes or no) and which features are active. The record does not include a name, email, user ID, shift dates or amounts. Only the App's admins can read it, and they use it as a summary of all devices. You can turn it off in Settings › Me and my account › Account and sync, and turning it off deletes this device's record. Error reports are part of these statistics: when something in the App fails, it sends the technical error message, the screen, the App version, the language and the type of system and browser, with no identifying detail and nothing you typed. Error reports are deleted after 30 days.
  • The leaderboard (only if you joined): the nickname, emoji, background and Plus badge you chose, and your best score in each game today, this week, this month, this year and of all time. This row is visible to all signed-in users, with the nickname you chose (for example a first name) and no email or work data. Leaving the table (or deleting the account) deletes it.
  • Competition with friends (only if you joined): the nickname and emoji you chose, the list of friends you accepted and, only if you chose to show them, this month's, this year's and all-time gross and your average pay per hour. This card is visible only to the friends on your list, with no real name, email, workplace or shift dates. An invitation to the competition is kept until it is accepted. If it is not accepted, it is deleted when your app opens after 14 days. Leaving the competition (or deleting the account) deletes the card.
  • Inviting friends to Shaati (only if you invited or were invited): your invitation code and, if you joined through someone's link, a record linking your account ID to theirs, with the date. The record is visible only to the two of you, to give each the free month, and holds no name, email or work data. The invitation code is deleted with the account; the record of who invited whom is kept after that (IDs only), so that each account is invited only once. A competition invitation you accepted is deleted when you leave the competition or delete the account. Your account ID (with no name, email or data) stays in the friend lists of people who were your friends in the competition, until they remove it.
  • Workplace code: when you share a workplace's rules, the cloud keeps only the workplace name and the pay rules you chose (and hourly pay only if you chose it), with no shifts, amounts or identifying details. Anyone who has the code can read them, and you can delete the code at any time.
  • Support requests (only if you wrote): the subject of the request, what you wrote, our correspondence, the first name you entered in the App, and if you chose, the App version, the kind of install (Android, website or app from the browser), and the operating system and browser with their main version numbers, without the phone model. If you wrote without signing in, also the email you entered so we can reply. Requests are kept in the cloud, visible only to you and the App's admins, and are deleted together with the account. A request made without an account is deleted up to 12 months after it was closed, or at once if you ask by email.

2. Where the information is kept and who can access it

On your device (browser storage), and after signing in, also in the App's cloud (Google Cloud Firestore, in the me-west1 region, Tel Aviv), linked to your account in the App. The database security rules block other users from accessing your data (except a leaderboard row, which is visible to signed-in users if you chose to join, and a friends' competition card, which is visible only to the friends you accepted). The Operator, as the owner of the database, has technical access, and uses it only to handle your request or a fault, or when the law requires it. Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and cloud storage is encrypted by Google.

3. What doesn't leave the phone

The API key, the lock code, the background picture for the pay card (checked on the phone and kept only there), the payslip files themselves (picture and PDF) and the full text recognized from them are kept only on the device, except what you approved to send for AI analysis (see section 5). Your location too, if you asked to choose a locality by it, is used only to find the nearest locality on the device, and is neither saved nor sent anywhere.

Unlocking with a fingerprint: the recognition is done entirely by the phone's own mechanism. The App does not receive, save or send fingerprints or face data; it saves only a technical ID that lets it ask the phone to confirm the identity.

3a. Cookies and what the browser keeps

The app uses no cookies, no ads and no tracking tools from other companies. The browser keeps only what the app needs to work: your data and settings (so it works offline), your sign-in, your language and display preferences, and a copy of the app itself. One thing is not needed: a random id for the usage statistics, kept only if you agreed and removable in Settings. When you sign in with Google or pay through Lemon Squeezy, their window keeps cookies under their own policy.

4. What the information is used for

Solely to provide the service: calculations, syncing between devices, notifications and insights. The usage statistics are used only to understand how many users there are and which features are worth improving. The information is not sold, not rented and not used for marketing or advertising.

5. Transfer to third parties

  • Google: sign-in authentication, cloud storage and hosting the website shaati.web.app (Firebase Hosting).
  • GitHub: hosting the App's code (without your data).
  • Google Calendar: only when you choose "Add to Google Calendar" on a shift: the role name, the shift times and the number of hours go in a link to Google's window, and are saved there only if you confirm.
  • Anthropic: only when you run an AI feature, with your key: in payslip analysis, the text you approved is sent after hiding the ID number, bank details, address, phone and email; in the assistant, the question is sent, and if you chose, a numeric summary from the App without identifying details. A photo of a work schedule you send to be read also goes to them, with the names in it, only so it can be read.
  • MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute): the weather for the opening screen's background. The coordinates of the locality you chose are sent, rounded to about 1 km, never your phone's location, at most once an hour and again when you change the locality. As with any internet request, they also receive the phone's IP address, the App's address and the ordinary browser details, with no name, account or cookies. They keep the IP address and the coordinates in their server logs in Oslo.
  • Code and font distribution servers (jsDelivr, cdnjs, cdn.sheetjs.com, Google Fonts) and the language data for text recognition (tessdata.projectnaptha.com): loading technical components, when you use a feature that needs them.
  • Google Play: only if you buy Shaati Plus in the Android app: the payment is made entirely with Google Play. The App checks with it whether the purchase exists.
  • Lemon Squeezy: only if you buy Shaati Plus on the website or iPhone: the payment, the receipt and the email address for the receipt are given to them directly. The App saves in your account only the activation code, and checks with them that it is valid.

We do not receive card or payment details. About payments and gifts, the usage statistics record only yes or no: whether Shaati Plus is active and where it was bought (Android or website), whether the Plus page was opened, whether a free trial started, whether you ever reached a free allowance, whether you gave a tip, whether you got a gift month for an invitation and whether a friend joined through your link. The same goes for opening the tax refund check.

Your data in the cloud is stored in Firestore in the Tel Aviv region (me-west1). Sign-in (Firebase Authentication) is processed on Google's servers in the United States. Anthropic, GitHub and Lemon Squeezy operate in the United States, MET Norway in Norway (in the European Economic Area), and the code distribution servers in several countries. The transfer is made with your consent, and subject to the providers' data protection commitments, under the Protection of Privacy Regulations (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad).

6. Your rights

You are not legally required to give us any information. Without an account you can use the App as a guest, and your data stays on the phone. Under the Protection of Privacy Law you have the right to see the information about you and to ask to correct or delete it. You can ask by email at lybtn48@gmail.com or through "Help and support" in the app, and we will answer within 30 days.

You can do most of this yourself in the App: view the information and correct it; export it (Settings › Backup, import and data › Backup, CSV export); and delete it permanently (Settings › Me and my account › Account and sync › Delete all data from the account). Signing out deletes the data from the device and keeps it in the account.

7. Information about others

Do not enter other people's personal information into the App (for example colleagues' details) without their consent.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information, but no system is completely secure. It's recommended to turn on a screen lock on the phone, and on a shared device, also the code lock in the App. If a serious security incident happens, we will report it to the Privacy Protection Authority at once and follow its instructions, including notifying you.

9. Keeping information

The information in the account is kept until you delete it or the account. Some information is deleted after a set time: a competition invitation that was not accepted is deleted when the sender's app opens after 14 days; a support request made without an account is deleted up to 12 months after it was closed; a statistics record not updated for 13 months is deleted (and so are the records of your other phones after the account is deleted). The record of who invited whom is kept, with IDs only, so that each account is invited only once. Backups you downloaded to a file are kept by you and are your responsibility.

10. Minors

The minimum age for use is 13. Anyone under 18 uses the App with the consent of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian may write to lybtn48@gmail.com to see their child's data, delete it or cancel the child's subscription. The date of birth is used only for the purposes in section 1.

11. Notifications

Notifications are sent from the device itself, only if you allowed them, and you can turn them off at any time in settings.

12. Changes to the policy

A material change will be shown in the App and will require renewed consent.

13. Contact

Privacy requests and questions, including a request to see, correct or delete your information: by email at lybtn48@gmail.com or through "Help and support" in the app (the question mark on the home screen). The project's issues page on GitHub is public, so do not send privacy requests through it.

14. The website shaati.web.app

The website has no cookies, ads or tracking. The browser keeps only the city you picked for the sky picture, whether you paused its motion, and that you read the storage notice. The calculator and the quick check run in the browser, and nothing typed in them is sent. The hosting service (Google's Firebase Hosting) records the IP address and browser details on every visit.
