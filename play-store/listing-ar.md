# Google Play listing: Arabic (ar)

App 1.34.0, written 6 Nov 2026 for Arabic-speaking workers in Israel (Arab citizens of Israel and East Jerusalem
residents working by the hour or in shifts). Modern Standard Arabic kept plain, with the app's own terms from
i18n/ar.json (وردية، قسيمة الراتب، بدل الاستجمام، الساعات الإضافية، جدول الأبطال) and the Hebrew words workers
see on the payslip in brackets (תלוש, דמי הבראה), because many read Hebrew at work and search with it. Every claim
was checked against src/ and matches listing-en.md. Counts printed by node on the exact strings inside the blocks
(`[...s].length`). Paste in Play Console › Main store listing › Manage translations › Arabic – ar.

ساعة الدوام والورديات
• اضغط عند البدء واضغط عند الانتهاء، مع الاستراحات، أو أضف وردية سابقة
• استيراد جدول العمل من صورة أو من رسالة واتساب، فتدخل الورديات إلى التقويم
• استيراد تقرير ساعات من العمل (Excel أو CSV أو PDF أو صورة) ومقارنته بورديّاتك قبل يوم الراتب

## Rejection rule (Misleading Claims, 25 Oct 2026)
- Second paragraph: private app, not linked to any government body, the three bodies named.
- "مصادر رسمية (بالعبرية)": the same 12 gov.il / btl.gov.il links as the Hebrew listing.
- "تقدير" (estimate) throughout; no "بالضبط", "حتى الشيكل" or "حسب القانون" as a promise.

## Accuracy notes
- Weekly rest: the app counts the Shabbat and Israel's holidays (Settings › rest days) and lets the worker mark any
  date as a rest day by hand (hebcal.js dayRules, type "force"). It has no setting for Friday or Sunday as the weekly
  rest day, which the law allows for non-Jewish workers. The listing says only what exists. Adding that option would
  make the app more useful for this audience.

## For a native speaker to check
- The name keeps "الورديات" and "قسيمة الراتب", the app's words. "التلوش" is common in speech but was left out of
  the name; the Hebrew "תלוש" sits in brackets in the first line.
- "الشبيبة العاملة" and "قانون عمل الشبيبة" follow the app; the formal name is "قانون عمل الأحداث".
- "الأجيرين" for employees (שכירים); some readers expect "العاملين بأجر".
- Mixed RTL text: Latin words (Shaati, Excel, PDF, API) and the Hebrew glosses render correctly in a bidi-aware
  field; check the Play preview once pasted.
