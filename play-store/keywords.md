# Keywords for the Play listing (he + en), and why each was chosen

Google Play has no keyword field: it reads the name, the short description and the full description. So each word
below sits in one of those, written into a real sentence (no lists of keywords, which Play treats as spam).

How they were picked (3 Nov 2026): the words in the brief; the Hebrew names and subtitles of the apps a worker
finds for the same need ("השכר שלי: ניהול משמרות ושכר", "שכרניק: מחשבון שכר", "משמרות", "אחראי משמרת",
"Timewatch שעון נוכחות", "שעון נוכחות זמן אמת"); Israeli consumer and rights sites people land on from Google
(jobcalc "מחשבון שעות עבודה", workrights, kolzchut, bizportal on payslips and holiday pay); and the words the app
itself uses on its screens. No paid ASO tool, no scraping of the stores. The real numbers come later from Play Console
› Statistics › Store listing acquisition, by search term.

## Hebrew

| Word | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| שעתי | name (first word), full | The Hebrew name under the icon on Hebrew phones; people who heard of the app type it. |
| Shaati | full (line 1) | The Latin name, used in links and on the website. |
| משמרות | name, short, full | The most common word in competing app names ("ניהול משמרות", "משמרות"); what a shift worker types first. |
| שכר / חישוב שכר / מחשבון שכר | name, short, full | "מחשבון שכר" is a competitor's subtitle; "חישוב שכר" is the everyday phrase. Both forms appear. |
| תלוש / תלוש שכר / תלוש משכורת | name, short, full | Payslip checking is what sets the app apart; both "תלוש שכר" and "תלוש משכורת" are said. |
| שעון נוכחות | short (first words), full | A very large search, mostly for employer systems (Timewatch and others). Placed first in the short description, as "שעון נוכחות אישי", so a worker looking for a personal clock sees it is one. Kept out of the name: much of that traffic wants their employer's app. |
| שבת / שבתות / חג | short, full | Shabbat pay is a top question of hourly workers, and the app counts it by the town's Shabbat times. |
| שעות נוספות | short, full | A high-intent pay question; competitors list it too. |
| דוח שעות | full | What workers get from work and import; jobcalc ranks for "מחשבון שעות עבודה". |
| סידור עבודה | full | The weekly schedule people photograph or get on WhatsApp; the import feature. |
| שכר מינימום / נוער עובד / חוק עבודת הנוער | full | Teen workers (a big share of hourly workers) search their rights. |
| נסיעות / דמי נסיעה | full | One of the common missing items on payslips. |
| דמי הבראה / דמי חגים / דמי מחלה / חופשה / פנסיה | full | Rights words people search on kolzchut and workrights; all are in the in-app guide. |
| החזר מס / טופס 106 | full | A strong search, and the app has a tax refund check. |
| נטו / ברוטו (הערכת נטו) | full | Workers want the take-home number; worded as an estimate. |
| ווידג'טים, וואטסאפ, אקסל, PDF | full | Words people use when looking for these exact features. |
| זכויות עובדים | full | The general rights search, the heading of a section. |

## English (Israelis with their phone in English)

| Word | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shaati | name | The brand. |
| shift tracker, pay tracker | name ("Shift & Pay Tracker") | The standard English app-category phrases (ShiftPal, WageFlow, Work Hours Tracker and many others use them). |
| hourly pay, Israel | short | Separates the app from the many US and global trackers: Israeli rules. |
| shift clock, time clock | short, full | The English for שעון נוכחות. |
| Shabbat, overtime | short, full | The Israeli pay premiums the app counts. |
| payslip, payslip check | short, full | Israeli English says payslip, not pay stub. |
| minimum wage, Youth Employment Law, recuperation pay, holiday pay, tax refund, Form 106 | full | Rights and tax terms as English-language Israeli sources write them. |

## Words left out on purpose
- "חינם", "free", "הכי טוב", "best", "#1", "חדש", "new", "הורידו": banned in Play listing text.
- "בדיוק", "exactly", "עד השקל", "לפי החוק" as a promise: the legal audit and Google's rejection of 25 Oct 2026
  (Misleading Claims). The texts say "הערכה" / "estimate" and "לפי כללי חוקי העבודה".
- Names of government bodies are used only in the disclaimer and the sources block, never as a selling point.
- Competitors' names (שכרניק, השכר שלי, Timewatch): using them in the listing breaks Play's rules on others' marks.
