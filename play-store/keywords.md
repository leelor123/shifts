# Keywords for the Play listing (he, en, ru, ar, fr, es, am), and why each was chosen

Google Play has no keyword field: it reads the name, the short description and the full description. So each word
below sits in one of those, written into a real sentence (no lists of keywords, which Play treats as spam).

How they were picked (3 Nov 2026): the words in the brief; the Hebrew names and subtitles of the apps a worker
finds for the same need ("השכר שלי: ניהול משמרות ושכר", "שכרניק: מחשבון שכר", "משמרות", "אחראי משמרת",
"Timewatch שעון נוכחות", "שעון נוכחות זמן אמת"); Israeli consumer and rights sites people land on from Google
(jobcalc "מחשבון שעות עבודה", workrights, kolzchut, bizportal on payslips and holiday pay); and the words the app
itself uses on its screens. No paid ASO tool, no scraping of the stores. The real numbers come later from Play Console
› Statistics › Store listing acquisition, by search term.

## Words left out on purpose
- "חינם", "free", "הכי טוב", "best", "#1", "חדש", "new", "הורידו": banned in Play listing text.
- "בדיוק", "exactly", "עד השקל", "לפי החוק" as a promise: the legal audit and Google's rejection of 25 Oct 2026
  (Misleading Claims). The texts say "הערכה" / "estimate" and "לפי כללי חוקי העבודה".
- Names of government bodies are used only in the disclaimer and the sources block, never as a selling point.
- Competitors' names (שכרניק, השכר שלי, Timewatch): using them in the listing breaks Play's rules on others' marks.
