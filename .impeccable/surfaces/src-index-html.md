Scope: the Shaati app (every screen of src/; Operate mode). Audience: hourly workers in Israel, many teens; job: start
or log a shift and see what it paid; check a payslip. Constraints: the opening show and the pull-to-refresh coin are
fixed; no flicker; six languages, RTL and LTR; light and dark; the user's accent colour setting keeps working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: pay counted like a good watch keeps time: exact, calm, valuable. It refuses the category default of a purple
gradient card with coloured glows, emoji and bouncing tiles.

OWN-WORLD: cream ground #f6f4ee (night #0e1118), white grouped cards on 1px hairlines, ink navy #0d2b6b for structure
and the place you are, gold #e9c987 only for value (the month's money, the summary, the active tab at night), go-green
#0b6b49 for the one daily action. One family, Rubik, tabular figures. Shadow only where something truly lifts.

STORY: open, see what this month paid so far, tap once to start the shift, trust the number because it looks exact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: greeting and date; any notice as one quiet row; the pay card (navy dial, gold label, the month's
gross large, three stats under a gold hairline); the green start button full width; shortcuts; recent shifts.

FORM: pinned by the brand (the 1.29 opening show's navy and gold dial); signature move: the gold hairline dial on the
pay card, its ticks filling with the month. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
